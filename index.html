⚡ Identity Shifting Game · Entry Rules

The Champion's Code

This isn't just an app — it's an identity shifting game. You're not playing a character. You're becoming one: the Champion already inside you. To enter, read each rule out loud, then tap to accept it.

0 / 6 accepted
1
Live the phrase

I live by the Inner Champion Philosophy: "I can and I will." Not "I hope." Not "I'll try." I can, and I will.

2
Choose your attitude

When things get hard, I pick one of my two powers: Resolution — "How could I fix this?" — or Gratitude — "How could I be grateful in this moment?"

3
Breathe through challenges

Emotional, mental, or physical — I overcome every challenge with my breathwork, just like we practice in the Academy.

4
Love yourself along the way

I am not allowed to speak poorly of others — and that means I may not speak poorly of myself either. I am on my own team.

5
Make one person smile a day

Every day, I challenge myself to make one person smile. (Champion's secret: it makes me smile too.)

6
The Champion's Mantra

Say this out loud — like you mean it — and tap after each time. Three times makes it real:

"I am so excited to discover how this becomes the best day of my life!"
